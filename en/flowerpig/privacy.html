Privacy policy

Operator: Malangsoft · Applies to Flower Pig (난다난다꽃돼지) for iOS and Android
Effective and last updated: November 11, 2026 · Contact: indian311@icloud.com

Gameplay and local storage

You can play the core game without creating a developer-managed account. Your best score, best height, number of runs, music, effects, vibration and tutorial preferences are stored on your device. Pending leaderboard submissions also store the relevant platform player identifier and score locally. There is no separate Malangsoft game server receiving these records.

Local records remain while app data is retained. Removing app data or uninstalling may remove them. Operating-system backup and restore settings may allow restoration.

Optional platform game services

A configured release can use Apple Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android to read your platform player identifier, submit scores and display a leaderboard. While signed in, completing a run attempts to submit the score for that account. In version 1.0, you can also choose to submit your best score. The game does not directly collect Apple or Google account passwords. Core gameplay and local records remain available without an online connection.

In test releases version 1.1.0(8) and later, the iOS app automatically requests Game Center authentication on its first screen. Android attempts to connect to Play Games at startup only when the locally stored time at which you turn 14 has been reached. For users under 14 or whose age has not been checked, Android starts neither the SDK nor the age-entry screen at launch. Users whose age has not been checked can choose sign-in in the records screen to enter their date of birth in initially blank fields. If authentication is cancelled or fails, core gameplay remains available as a guest. Scores earned while connected to an account are submitted automatically when a run ends. Failed submissions remain on your device separately for each account and are automatically retried when that account signs in again, the app restarts or the app returns to the foreground. These test releases have no manual best-score submission button. Guest records remain on your device and are not automatically assigned or sent to an account you sign in to later or to another account. These changes do not retroactively change the behavior of the production release version 1.0.0(7).

Platform providers handle account, gameplay and service information under their own policies. Profile and score visibility follows platform policies and settings. When you use online connection features on Android, the Google Play Games SDK automatically processes analytics and diagnostics for service stability and improvement. Scores are sent to your connected game account, and Google Play Games service data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. The game has no separate advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. This does not mean its platform SDKs process no information.

Android online connection and age checks

On Android version 1.0.0(7) and later, choosing to connect an online game account for the first time opens a date-of-birth screen. The input is processed only on your device and is not transmitted. Instead of retaining the original date-of-birth input, the app stores only the time at which you turn 14 in app-private device storage. In the South Korean release, the app does not initialize the Google Play Games SDK or perform sign-in, score submission or leaderboard operations for users under 14 or whose age has not been checked. You can choose to connect after the stored eligibility time is reached. Cancelling the age check or declining online connection does not prevent core gameplay or access to local records. Removing app data may also remove this age-check information.

Score sharing

Choosing Share passes a message containing your score to the system share sheet. You choose the recipient and destination app. Cancelling does not make the game directly send that message to another person. The selected app's privacy policy applies to subsequent handling.

Permissions and tracking

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Android internet permission supports online game services; vibration supports optional hit feedback. The current implementation has no in-game advertising, in-app purchases, advertising-identifier tracking or targeted ads.

Retention and deletion

Removing app data or uninstalling affects local records. Leaderboard records already sent to a platform are managed by Apple or Google and are not guaranteed to be deleted by uninstalling. Use the platform's game-data or profile controls, or contact the platform's support service, to manage or request deletion. There is no separately registered Malangsoft game account.

TestFlight testing

When you use a TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects installation and usage information and crash diagnostics and shares it with the app provider. This can include your invitation email, app and OS versions, device model and session information. Feedback you choose to send may include comments and screenshots. Malangsoft uses beta information to investigate issues and improve the app; access or deletion requests can be sent to the support address below. This does not add a separate behavioral analytics SDK to the game. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Contact and updates

Email indian311@icloud.com for privacy questions or support. Your email address and correspondence are used as needed to handle the inquiry. Requests about retention and deletion are welcome at the same address. Include the app name and request, without passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal details. Parents or guardians may also contact us.

We update this page and its date when the app's features or platform data practices change.

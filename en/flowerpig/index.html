GOOD TO KNOW.

Easy to get started.

Do I need an internet connection?

Core gameplay and your local best record work offline. Online leaderboards require an account, a network connection and a release configured for Game Center or Google Play Games. Online services and store distribution are currently being prepared.

Can I turn off sound or vibration?

Music, effects and vibration have separate settings. You can also pause a run and return to it.

Does a bigger screen give an advantage?

Clouds fill the screen, while lanes, speed and collision rules use the same game world. Extra screen space does not reveal approaching obstacles earlier.

Where can I download it?

Public store downloads are not available yet. iOS TestFlight testing and App Store and Google Play submissions are being prepared.
